Students: Reg. Number as username  ·  Login Help & Examples
📋 How to Log In — Step by Step
Step 1 — Username
Enter your Registration Number exactly as it appears on your admission letter.
SN/NSU/25/0020  or  AH/NRT/24/0093
Step 2 — Arrange Your Name
Write your name in this order: First name → Middle name → Surname
Use the name exactly as it appears on your admission letter.
Step 3 — Build Your Password
Your password has 3 parts joined together (no spaces):
Part A First 2 digits of your reg. number
Part B Last 4 digits of your reg. number
Part C First letter of each name (CAPITALS)
✦ Worked Examples
Student with 2 names
Name: Christian Frimpong  |  Reg: SN/NSU/25/0020
Digits in reg: 250020 → First 2 = 25, Last 4 = 0020
Initials: Christian + Frimpong = CF
Password = 250020CF
Student with 3 names (first + middle + surname)
Name: Mercy Akomabea Adu  |  Reg: SN/NSU/25/0004
Digits in reg: 250004 → First 2 = 25, Last 4 = 0004
Initials: Mercy + Akomabea + Adu = MAA
Password = 250004MAA
Student with 3 names (Level 200 / year 24)
Name: Obed Owoahene Acheampong  |  Reg: SN/NSU/25/0010
Digits in reg: 250010 → First 2 = 25, Last 4 = 0010
Initials: Obed + Owoahene + Acheampong = OOA
Password = 250010OOA
⚠️ Passwords are case-sensitive. Use CAPITAL letters for the initials.
Contact the General Office if you cannot log in after following these steps.
