Portal Services

Everything You Need in One Place

UCC Nyakrom Smart Campus Portal brings together all essential student and administrative services.

🏠

Accommodation Management

View your room assignment, check roommates, apply for accommodation, and track your application status — all from your phone.

📅

Teaching Timetable

Access your weekly timetable, block lecture schedule, and weekly block timetable for all programmes — updated in real time.

🔧

Maintenance Reporting

Report electrical, plumbing, structural, or cleaning issues in your room and track the resolution status in real time.

📢

Notice Board

Stay informed with urgent and general notices from the General Office and campus management, accessible on every login.

🏛️

Campus Facilities

Browse available campus facilities, view their status and availability, and report any facility-related issues to management.

📋

Online Applications

New students can apply for campus accommodation online, select a preferred room, and receive updates without visiting the office.

How to Access
Log in with your Registration Number and default password, or apply below if you are new.
