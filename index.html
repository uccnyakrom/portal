About the Campus

Excellence in Healthcare & Science Education

The Agona Nyakrom Campus of the University of Cape Coast is a premier institution dedicated to training world-class health professionals in Ghana's Central Region. We offer rigorous academic programmes in Nursing and Nutrition.

🏥
Clinical Training
State-of-the-art simulation labs and affiliated teaching hospitals.
🌿
Green Campus
Lush, serene environment designed for focused learning.
💻
Smart Systems
Integrated digital portals for seamless administration.
🏠
Residential Life
Fully residential for nursing and nutrition programme students.
Academic Programmes

Train. Heal. Lead.

Our carefully designed health science programmes combine rigorous theory with immersive clinical practice to produce confident, compassionate professionals.

School of Allied Health Sciences
BSc Nursing
A four-year programme developing skilled nurses ready for complex clinical environments across Ghana and beyond.
📋 Level 100 – 400
School of Allied Health Sciences
BSc Nutrition & Dietetics
Equipping students with the science of food, health, and therapeutic nutrition for community and clinical settings.
🥗 Level 100 – 400
